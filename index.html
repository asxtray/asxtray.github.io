What is a VPN?

A Virtual Private Network (VPN) creates a secure, encrypted connection between your device and the internet. It routes your internet traffic through a remote server, hiding your IP address and protecting your data from hackers, ISPs, and other third parties.

How does Safe-tunnel VPN work?

Safe-tunnel VPN uses advanced encryption protocols to create a secure tunnel between your device and our servers. When you connect to our VPN, all your internet traffic is encrypted and routed through this tunnel, making it impossible for anyone to intercept or monitor your online activities.

What devices are supported?

Safe-tunnel VPN supports Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, and Linux. You can protect all your devices with a single subscription, depending on your plan.

How do I install Safe-tunnel VPN?

Installing Safe-tunnel VPN is simple. After subscribing, download the app from our website or your device's app store. Run the installer and follow the on-screen instructions. Once installed, log in with your credentials and connect to any of our servers with a single click.
